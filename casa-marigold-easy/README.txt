Casa Marigold

1. Unzip the folder.
2. Double-click index.html. That is your website.
3. Double-click admin.html (or click "Staff login" at the bottom of the site) to edit the menu.

First time in admin you choose a password.

To publish changes to everyone:
In admin click "Download menu.js", replace the menu.js in this folder with it,
then upload the folder to your host (for example drag it onto netlify.com/drop).
